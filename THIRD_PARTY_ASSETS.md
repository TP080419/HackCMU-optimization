# Third-party assets

No third-party visual files ship in XenoFlow 1.0. The game uses its original geometric Canvas renderer.

The two approved optional packs were each downloaded once and inspected during the final visual pass:

| Pack | Official source | Version inspected | License | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kenney Tiny Factory | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-factory | 1.0 (2026) | Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) | Not bundled; its 16×16 pixel palette and silhouettes were less legible when mixed into the established 32px dark industrial renderer. |
| Kenney Tiny Farm | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-farm | 1.0 (2026) | Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) | Not bundled; its warm farm palette was visually inconsistent with the alien industrial map. |

The license text included with each downloaded archive states that the content is free for personal, educational, and commercial projects and links to https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Review archives were kept outside tracked project assets and are not required at runtime.
